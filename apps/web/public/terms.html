What GoodCash is

A tool to cash out G$ to naira. You connect your own wallet and you confirm every step. GoodCash does not hold your funds and cannot move them without your confirmation.

Who can use it

  • You are 18 or older and the wallet and bank account are yours.
  • You use it lawfully, where you live.

Costs and amounts

  • You see an estimate before you confirm. The final naira amount depends on the exchange rate when the payout is made, so it can differ a little from the estimate.
  • The costs are the GoodDollar contribution (set by the GoodDollar protocol), the payout partner's fee, and a small network fee. GoodCash charges its own fee of 1% of each cash out, shown on the review screen before you confirm. If that changes, we will show the new fee before you confirm.
  • There is a minimum cash out. The app tells you what it is.

Payouts and problems

  • The naira is paid by a payout partner to the bank account you entered. Check the details carefully: we cannot reverse a payment to a wrong account that you entered.
  • If a payout cannot be completed, the partner returns the funds to your wallet.
  • Network and bank delays can happen. If something looks wrong, use Contact support in the app.

Risks

Crypto prices and exchange rates change. Blockchain transactions cannot be undone. Never share your seed phrase. Using any wallet or network carries risk, and you are responsible for your own wallet.
