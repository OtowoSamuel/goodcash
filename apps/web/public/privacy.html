Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

We collect as little as we can. This page says what that is.

What we handle

  • Your wallet address. It is public on the blockchain. We use it to show your cash outs and to find unfinished ones.
  • Your bank details (bank, account number, account name). You type them in so your naira can be paid. They go through our server to our payout partner. We do not keep them. They are not saved in your browser or in our storage.
  • Your email, only if you give it: to email you when a payout is sent, or when you ask for help. A payout-email address is kept for up to 3 days. A help request (your email, your message and the cash out it is about) is kept for 90 days.
  • Your ID details when you add G$ with naira (full name, date of birth, and your BVN or NIN). The law requires our payment partner to know who is paying in naira. These details go through our server straight to that partner, once, for that order. We do not keep them, and they are never written to our logs or saved in your browser.
  • A short-lived fingerprint. To stop the same cash out being paid out twice, we keep a one-way scrambled code made from the bank and account number you chose and your wallet, for 3 hours. It cannot be turned back into your account number.
  • Cash out records. We keep the link between a swap and its payout order, and its status, so we can show it and help you. These contain no bank details.
  • Add G$ and CELO records. For each order to add G$ or CELO with naira we keep your wallet address, the naira amount, which one you bought, the date and the order's status, for about 13 months, so we can show it in your History. These contain no ID or bank details.
  • Technical logs. Error reports contain a short error code and a cash out or transaction number, never bank details or your email. Our hosting provider also sees the usual connection details such as your IP address to run the service and block abuse.

On your device

Your browser stores your recent cash outs (amounts, transaction numbers), your last wallet, and your support email if you typed one. Use Disconnect wallet or clear your browser data to remove it.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the service.
  • Paycrest is our payout partner. It receives the bank details and the cash out amount to pay you. When you add G$ with naira, it also receives your ID details and the bank account you pay from. It applies its own checks and privacy terms.
  • Resend sends the emails you ask for.
  • Your wallet app and the Celo network are run by others. Transactions on the blockchain are public and cannot be erased.

We do not

  • sell your data, or show ads, or track you across other sites.

Your choices

To ask us to delete a help request or a payout email, or to ask what we hold about a cash out, use Contact support in the app. Some records, such as blockchain transactions, cannot be changed by anyone.
