Where these numbers come from

Cash outs come from the public records of our contracts on the Celo network, the same records anyone can open on the block explorer. Top-ups are a running count we keep: each confirmed naira payment is added once, as a count and an amount. No name or bank detail is kept in these counts or shown here, and no partner is named. Verified people are checked with GoodDollar. Its first verification lasts 3 days and the next one 180 days, so everyone GoodDollar has verified is counted, even if they need to verify again. The page refreshes about every 10 minutes.
