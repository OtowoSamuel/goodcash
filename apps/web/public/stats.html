Verified people

GoodCash is for GoodDollar-verified people. Wallets are counted by person: several wallets that belong to one verified person count once. The verify step started on 20 Nov 2026, so wallets used before then may not be verified.

Where these numbers come from

Cash outs are read from the public records of our two contracts on the Celo network, the same records anyone can open on the block explorer. Top-ups are a running count we keep: each confirmed naira payment is added once, as a count and an amount in naira. No name, bank detail or wallet is kept in that count or shown here. People and partner numbers are counts only; no wallet or partner is named. Weeks start on Monday (UTC). Wallets are shortened. The page refreshes about every 10 minutes.
