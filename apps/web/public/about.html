About GoodCash

goodcash.xyz

GoodCash lets people turn their GoodDollar (G$) into naira in their Nigerian bank account, in a few taps.

How it works

  • You connect your own wallet. We never hold your G$ or your money.
  • You confirm the cash out in your wallet. Your G$ is swapped on the Celo network.
  • A payout partner turns the proceeds into naira and sends them to the bank account you chose.

What we do and do not do

  • Non-custodial. Your funds stay in your wallet until you confirm each step. GoodCash cannot move them for you.
  • No hidden fee. GoodCash does not currently charge a fee. The costs you see before you confirm are the GoodDollar contribution, the payout partner's fee and the network fee.
  • We will never ask for your wallet's seed phrase or private key, a password, or a transfer to a personal account. If anyone does, it is not us.

Check it for yourself

  • Our swap contract is verified on the Celo block explorer: view the contract.
  • Our code is public: github.com/OtowoSamuel/goodcash.

Contact

For help with a cash out, open the app, go to History, tap your cash out and choose Contact support. To report a security problem, see our security contact.

GoodCash is independent software. It is not affiliated with the GoodDollar Foundation.
