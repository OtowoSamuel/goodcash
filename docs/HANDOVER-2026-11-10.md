## Built today
- **Live keys by email link.** `node scripts/partners.mjs approve-live <id>` only queues the partner (`liveapprove:queue`).
  The 10-minute schedule makes the keys, emails the partner's confirmed address a 24-hour one-time link
  (page: /docs/get-live-keys/confirm/, endpoint POST /v1/live-keys/confirm). Nothing is printed or sent by hand.
  `--print` keeps the old way; `--rotate` revokes both live keys, then queues new ones. Approving also switches top-ups on
  unless they were switched off on purpose. Not yet seen end to end with a real inbox.
- **Fewer KV list calls.** The schedule lists only while a marker (`work:<name>`) says something is open, plus once an hour.
  Code: services/orders/src/v1/work.ts. Lists were 907 of 1,000 free per day on 10 Nov (reads 11.5k of 100k, writes 193 of
  1,000); check the usage page after the 00:00 UTC reset. The $5 Workers Paid plan is the backstop (decision pending).
- **Widget resumes a paid top-up.** On wallet connect it asks /history for a paid order whose last step never ran and opens
  "Get your G$". Fixed the real ₦2,028 customer (MetaMask).
- **OKX Wallet.** The connect screen asks first when OKX is in the browser (laptop extension or the OKX app's browser) and
  offers "Open in OKX Wallet" on phones (okx://wallet/dapp/url deep link). Magic's own wallet list cannot show OKX.
  A user confirmed it connects. Not tested: Celo and Base signing through OKX, or OKX on a partner page.
