# GoodCash for Partners

**Let your users cash out GoodDollar (G$) to their Nigerian bank account, without leaving your app.**

## What your users get
- Type an amount, pick their bank, see their own name to confirm, tap once in their wallet.
- Naira in their bank account, usually within minutes.
- Every fee shown up front, in plain words, before they confirm.

## What you get
- A "Cash out to bank" button in your app or game, live in about **10 minutes** (one script tag), or a fully
  custom flow with our SDK and API in a day or two.
- Works for web, WebGL and Unity games, React and React Native apps, and server payouts (fintechs, games paying
  players from their own wallet).
- Optional **revenue share**: add your own fee (up to 3%), paid to you monthly.
- Signed webhooks when payouts land, a full test mode with no real money, and plain error messages.

## Safety
- **Non-custodial:** GoodCash never holds your users' money or keys. The user's own wallet (or your server wallet)
  signs one transaction.
- Our contract is verified and public; it pays the payout partner exactly and returns any surplus to the user in
  the same transaction. It holds nothing.
- The SDK checks every transaction before a wallet signs it.
