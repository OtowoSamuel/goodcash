Rules that keep it clean

Color jobsPurple = main action and progress. Yellow = sticker, coin and cost highlight. Mint = money you receive and success. Nothing else.
One stickerAt most one tilted element per screen (the coin or the badge). Everything else sits straight.
Outlines2.5px black on cards, buttons and chips. Hard shadow 5px on cards and buttons, 3px on small chips.
SurfacesLilac #cdb8ff background, plain white cards. No gradients or glow.
TypeBricolage Grotesque 800 for numbers and titles, 600 for body. Numbers use tabular figures. Addresses and receipts can use Inter.
Buttons52 to 54px, 16px radius, purple with white text. Disabled is pale lilac with no shadow and says why.
